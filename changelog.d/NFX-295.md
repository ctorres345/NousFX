### Sync
- `nousfx_sync.py` ships NousBoard from its local clone (`~/Projects/NousBoard`, or `[nousboard] path` in `local.toml`): the `board` skill link, the `~/.local/bin/board` CLI link and the SessionStart/Stop unread hook. It takes over the entries `install-local.sh` wrote without doubling them. With no clone it prints one note and removes what an earlier sync made (NFX-295).
