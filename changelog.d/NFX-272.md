### Review gate
- A watch-item ledger, so a retro counts a mistake across tickets and projects (NFX-272). `session_report.py watch add` appends a row (slug, ticket, pointer evidence, one-line note, project, date) to `<root>/<project>/watch.jsonl` with `--project`, or to the literal `<root>/NousFX/watch.jsonl` with `--harness`; inside NousFX (the `scripts/nousfx_sync.py` marker, not the folder name) every row is a harness row. A new slug needs `--new` and the refusal lists every existing slug with its first note (its meaning); a slug lives in one ledger; a repeat ticket, prose evidence and secret-shaped values are refused, all in one refusal. `watch add --merge OLD --into NEW` appends a merge row, never a rewrite. `watch list [--min N]` prints each slug with its distinct `ticket@project` count, most first, `class` at 2 or more, harness slugs counted across all projects, and the slug's first note as its meaning; with no ledger it says so and exits 0; a torn line, and a row from a newer script version (`v`), are skipped and reported. The retro reads `watch list` before grouping and adds its watch items, `--harness` when the fix belongs in NousFX (`after-merge/references/retro.md`). Past instance: the testfail-edit mistake in NFX-214, 218, 216 and 236, logged "seen once" four times.
