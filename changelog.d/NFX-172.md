### Via assets
- The billed Gemini API can make an image when agy runs out of image quota (NFX-172). It is off by default and turned on per project in that project's allow-list entry: `"assets": {"image_fallback": "api", "image_model": "gemini-3.1-flash-image"}`, with `gemini` also in its destinations. `generate` then makes that one image through `gemini-api image -m <model>`. The prompt goes over stdin from an empty folder, and gemini-api reads its key from the OS keyring only, as Argon's billed route does (NFX-164). The key is never read, passed or printed. stderr says the image is billed before the call. The JSON gives `route` (`agy` or `gemini-api`) and `model`, and `via_assets_review.py add --origin "gemini-api <model>"` records them in the state, the gallery and the log. `plan` reports `image_fallback` with `billed_max`, the most images the batch could bill. SVGs never fall back. With the fallback off, or `gemini` not allowed, a quota stop is still exit 21 and gemini-api is never started. A failed billed call is never retried: exit 23 when gemini-api is missing or its key is not in the keyring, exit 1 otherwise.
