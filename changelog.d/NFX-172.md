### Via assets
- The billed Gemini API can make an image when agy runs out of image quota (NFX-172). It is off by default and turned on per project in that project's allow-list entry: `"assets": {"image_fallback": "api", "image_model": "gemini-3.1-flash-image"}`, with `gemini` also in its destinations. `generate` then makes that one image through `gemini-api image -m <model>`. The prompt goes over stdin from an empty folder, and gemini-api reads its key from the OS keyring only, as Argon's billed route does (NFX-164). The key is never read, passed or printed. stderr says the image is billed before the call. The JSON gives `route` (`agy` or `gemini-api`) and `model`, and `via_assets_review.py add --origin "gemini-api <model>"` records them in the state, the gallery and the log. `plan` reports `image_fallback` with `billed_max`, the most images the batch could bill. SVGs never fall back. With the fallback off, or `gemini` not allowed, a quota stop is still exit 21 and gemini-api is never started. Any fallback failure (gemini-api missing, its key not in the keyring, or the billed call failing, timing out or returning no image) is exit 23 and stops the batch, so the next image is not billed again. It is never retried.
